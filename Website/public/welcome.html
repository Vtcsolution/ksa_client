An overdue follow-up, a seriously unhappy review, a new website inquiry nobody's claimed yet — none of it waits for someone to go looking. 5

See the AI actually at work

Four specific places the system is doing work for you, in plain terms:

Feedback

Reads the words, not just the star rating

The moment a review comes in — like the one you left in Step 2 — the AI reads the actual sentence, not just the number of stars, and decides how serious it is. Even a 5-star review with a buried complaint gets caught, and it's written up in Arabic and English with a suggested next step.

Find it under Customer Feedback in the dashboard.

Calls

Every sales call, summarized automatically

Once your phone line is connected, the AI listens to each recorded call, writes a summary, and scores how likely that customer is to sign — so a manager can review a call in 10 seconds instead of listening to all of it.

Find it under Call Intelligence in the dashboard.

Meetings

One line of notes becomes a full recap

When a rep finishes a meeting, they jot a single quick note. The AI turns that into a clean summary — the client's mood and a recommended next step — saved directly on that client's file.

Appears automatically inside any client's record after a meeting is marked done.

Reports

A written summary of each rep's week

Instead of reading raw numbers, click one button to get a short written narrative of how a rep's week went, plus specific, concrete coaching advice.

Find the Generate AI Summary button on the Weekly Report page.
